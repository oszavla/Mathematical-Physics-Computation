\section*{Introduction}
The regular representation of $S_4$ (symmetric group on 4 elements) consists of 24 permutation matrices of size $24 \times 24$, each corresponding to an element of the group.

\section*{Matrix Notation}
Each matrix $M_g$ for $g \in S_4$ acts on the vector space with basis indexed by group elements. The entry $M_g(i,j) = 1$ if $g \cdot h_i = h_j$, where $h_i, h_j$ are group elements in some fixed order.
